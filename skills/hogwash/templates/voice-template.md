# Voice profile (placeholder)

Host agent: this is a seed, not a profile. Build the real one with the
`idiolect` skill, which captures a voice as evidence-backed mechanics: a
portrait, sixteen scored dimensions, punctuation and function-word habits, a
lexicon, and per-format overlays in a `registers/` directory beside this file.
Without that skill, fill the sections below from the user's own writing or
their explicit answers — never from generated text.

## Portrait

One paragraph: who is talking, to whom, and what reading them feels like.

## Mechanics

Checkable rules only:

- Sentence rhythm and length variation (state the spread, not just "punchy").
- Contractions policy.
- Punctuation habits: em-dash, semicolon, parentheses, exclamation marks.
- Paragraph shape. Lists vs prose. Emphasis and emoji.
- Tense, active or passive, first-person and reader-address habits.
- The order of claims, evidence, examples, and conclusions.

Mark absolute constraints as `Hard rule`, habits as `Tendency` with how often
they apply. Never invent a fact, number, quotation, or anecdote while matching
this voice.

## Lexicon

- Words the writer reaches for (options, never quotas).
- Words the writer avoids, each with a replacement.
- Dialect and idiom to preserve, not normalize.

End with one question that separates the real voice from a plausible
imitation.
