# Margin — notes that stay where you put them

Margin is a note-taking app for people who are tired of their notes moving.

## Plain files, plain folders

Every note is a markdown file on your disk. No database, no proprietary
container, no export button. Open the folder in Finder and the notes are
there, named what you named them. If Margin disappears tomorrow, you still
have everything.

## Fast enough to think in

Search returns in under 30 milliseconds on a library of 20,000 notes. We
measured this on a 2021 MacBook Air, not a workstation. Opening the app takes
about 200 milliseconds from cold.

## Links without ceremony

Type `[[` and start typing a note title. Margin completes it. Backlinks appear
at the bottom of every note automatically, and you can see the whole graph if
that is the kind of thing you enjoy, though most people look at it once.

## What Margin does not do

No AI summaries. No collaborative editing. No mobile app yet — it is on the
roadmap for spring, and we would rather ship a good one late than a bad one
now. No sync service of our own; point it at a folder in iCloud, Dropbox, or a
git repository and use what you already trust.
